Why MBench?

Existing video-generation benchmarks often focus on visual quality, motion coherence, or prompt alignment. MBench instead asks a world-model question: can a generated world remember what should remain stable, what should evolve, and what causal consequences should persist?

E

Entity Consistency

Tracks object geometry, texture, human identity, and appearance across occlusion and re-entry.

V

Environment Consistency

Measures whether scene layout, projection, lighting, and rendering style remain stable over time.

C

Causal Consistency

Evaluates self-evolution, interaction, instruction following, and action-conditioned dynamics.

Benchmark Taxonomy

Three-level memory evaluation hierarchy

The page reserves space for your final Figure 1, but also provides editable cards for fast explanation before the paper figures are finalized.

Entity Consistency
  • Object: geometry consistency, texture consistency.
  • Human: identity consistency, appearance consistency.
Environment Consistency
  • Spatial: epipolar geometry, reprojection consistency.
  • Rendering: lighting consistency, style consistency.
Causal Consistency
  • Self-evolution: trigger, physical/event correctness.
  • Interaction: text-conditioned and action-conditioned instruction following.

Evaluation Protocol

Trigger-Conditioned Scoring

MBench separates “the model actually entered a memory-demanding case” from “the model remained consistent after that case happened,” reducing inflated scores from static or conservative generations.

01

Curate long videos

Collect real-captured clips with occlusion, camera motion, state changes, and interactions.

02

Generate prompts/actions

Create text-conditioned continuation prompts and action-conditioned exit–wait–reenter sequences.

03

Verify triggers

Use VLM checks to verify whether the memory-triggering event actually occurs.

04

Score consistency

Compute reliability only on triggered samples, then combine reliability and coverage into M-Score.

M-Scorek = 2 × Srelk × Ctrigk / (Srelk + Ctrigk)
